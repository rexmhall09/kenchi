# Data

Kenchi 1 was pretrained on about 7.06 billion tokens, then fine-tuned on
conversations with and without tools. The training data is not included here.

Conversation examples were also made by combining earlier questions and
answers, inventing names, and varying tool names and result formats. Arithmetic
examples were computed by script.

## Built with Qwen

Built with Qwen. The SmolTalk subsets and some function-calling rows used here
were generated by [Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct).
The short lines about the name, the four values, small talk and limits were
written by Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, running locally. I selected lines from its output
without rewording them. Both models are listed as Apache-2.0.

## Fern

The short template lines for identity, values, abstention wording and
guess-on-request were written by Fern 0.1 (Mirendil) and then pruned by
selection, 385 lines down to 227. Nothing was reworded. They are used in about
9,000 short examples. I could not find Fern's terms on training with its
outputs, so no longer text from it is in the data.
